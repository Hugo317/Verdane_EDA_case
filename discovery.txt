    Q1.1 - Even with that dip, does free shipping increase profit overall?

        Average order value: free = 2.67x no-free
        Average spend: free = 3.01x no-free
        Average profit: free = 3.06x no-free

        After their first free-shipping order a customer is almost 2x
        more valuable. First strong insight.
